# Hood Warfare Monopoly
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Delegated under user's creative liberty: React/Vite client and Node/Socket.IO authoritative server, deployed with Railway CLI.
## Users
Friends playing private online matches. Online first confirmed by user; solo with bots also planned.
## Product Purpose
A complete property trading party game inspired by the energy and turf battles of Hood Warfare, with original setting and artwork.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Rent debt can trigger optional one versus one shootouts. Roulette special cards can send players to jail, grant escape, or award jackpots. Private invite rooms, bots, reconnection, upgrades, trading, elimination and match victory. In-game currency only. Work in ~/Desktop/hw-monopoly. Deploy on Railway.
## Brand Commitments
Street board game with creative liberty. Original crews and city. Working title: Hood Warfare Monopoly; display title: Spin the Block.
## Product Principles
Online reliability comes first. Every risk communicates its consequences. Turns stay moving. Delight through authored board art and meaningful choices.
