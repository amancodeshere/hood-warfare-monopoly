# Hood Warfare Monopoly

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated under user's creative liberty: React/Vite client and Node/Socket.IO authoritative server, deployed with Railway CLI.

## Users

Friends playing private online matches. Online first confirmed by user; solo with 1–7 bots also available.

## Product Purpose

A complete property trading party game inspired by the energy and turf battles of Hood Warfare, with original setting and artwork.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Rent debt can trigger optional one versus one shootouts. Roulette special cards can send players to jail, grant escape, or award jackpots. Private/public rooms for 2–8 crews, quick matchmaking, bots/autofill, spectators, reconnection, auctions, utilities, four houses plus hotels, teams and private team chat, multi-asset trading, rescue gifts, personal turn clocks, profiles/friends/invitations/history/earned cosmetics, elimination and rematches. Default winner takes all; optional round caps. Expanded 40-square maps and Oppa Stoppa’s fair 1–10% cash spin fund the Community Plot. Existing saved rooms retain their original 28-square board. In-game currency only. Work in ~/Desktop/hw-monopoly. Deploy on Railway.

## Brand Commitments

Street board game with creative liberty. Original crews and city. Working title: Hood Warfare Monopoly; display title: Spin the Block.

## Product Principles

Online reliability comes first. Every risk communicates its consequences. Turns stay moving. Delight through authored board art and meaningful choices.

## Accounts and map authorship

The login entry supports guest continuation and username/password accounts. Guests host two base cities and may join any selected city. Real email verification unlocks four extra city themes and up to ten personal saved maps. Map Studio edits square names, district names/colors, and surprise-space event types while keeping essential spaces and prices balanced. Room maps are snapshots, including rematches. Email delivery requires a verified Resend sender and private Railway credentials; unavailable delivery must be disclosed. Email verification is not password recovery.

## Live rent duels and street pieces

Shootouts use a live 2D first-person alley view with server-checked aiming, dodging, cover, ammunition, reloads, headshots, health, bot play, and a 30-second cap. Exact ties protect the landlord. Existing saved tactic duels retain their original rules. The board makes ownership explicit with named badges and mortgage status, larger hooded crew figures with earned emblems, readable illustrated house counts and hotels, and full resident lists on inspected squares.
